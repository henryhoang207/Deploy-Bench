\section*{Data availability}
% src: README.md; DATA_ACCESS.md; LICENSING.md; THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md; reproducibility/registrations/manifest.json
The reproducibility package is available at
\url{https://github.com/henryhoang207/Deploy-Bench/releases/tag/speechcomm-v1.0.0}.
It contains utterance-level scores, frozen thresholds, evaluation manifests, the two registration snapshots and
deviation log, analysis and table-generation code, supporting metadata, and the author-trained round-2 checkpoints.
Author-written code is licensed under MIT; original documentation and author-trained checkpoints are licensed under
CC BY 4.0. Third-party components retain their original licences. Dataset-derived scores and metadata follow the
corpus-specific terms documented in the package, including attribution requirements for ASVspoof, CC BY-SA 4.0
for In-the-Wild, and CC BY-NC 4.0 for MLAAD-derived material. Source audio and upstream checkpoints are obtained
from their original providers. Access to MLAAD audio requires a Hugging Face account and acceptance of the
provider's access conditions. The package itself is downloadable without registration or approval; its
\texttt{DATA\_ACCESS.md}, \texttt{LICENSING.md}, and third-party notices give the source links and applicable terms.
